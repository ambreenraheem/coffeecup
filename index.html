Our Story

We Brew More Than
Just Coffee

CoffeeCup was born on the streets of Defence, Karachi — a neighbourhood that deserved more than instant coffee and generic café chains. We believed the city was ready for something real.

We source premium single-origin beans from Ethiopia, Colombia, and Indonesia, roasted in small batches to honour each bean's character. Every cup that leaves our counter is a conversation between origin, craft, and the person holding it.

Located in the heart of DHA Phase V, we are more than a café. We are a ritual, a pause, a moment of warmth in a fast-moving city.
